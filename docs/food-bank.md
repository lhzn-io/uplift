# Anchor Deployment: Food Bank Operations

This document describes the context and variables for the regional food banking deployment.

## Goal
Measure the impact of real-time logistics routing guidance via the Uplift reasoning engine towards decreasing spoilage.

## Metrics
- **Treatment:** Logistic team members utilizing ZeroClaw AI routing.
- **Control:** Standard legacy dynamic routing.
- **Outcome:** Percent of perishable inventory delivered pre-spoilage.

Aligns with experimental schemas under `docs/methodology.md`.
